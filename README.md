The lava texture tiling was added to enhance the effect that the scene is above a pool of hot lava. I made this by multiplying a glow integer to time and adding them to tiling and offset along with the texture UV. I then added a flow map time to enhance the movement of the tiling to juice the lava realism. Overall this wasn't too bad and the glowing effect and movement on the shader really sells the heat in the scene.

Adding the multiple UV textures was much easier to implement and resulted in a platform that players can easily differentiate on it being safe to walk on. This enhances the scene by having more dark contrast from the lava and looks ground like. To make the ground present as walkable, I added two texture UV's, used the blend node and changed the opacity to create the illusion that it is different from the lava and is walkable.
