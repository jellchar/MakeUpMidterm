The lava texture tiling was added to enhance the effect that the scene is above a pool of hot lava. I made this by multiplying a glow integer to time and adding them to tiling and offset along with the texture UV. Overall this wasn't too bad and the glowing effect on the shader really sells the heat in the scene.
